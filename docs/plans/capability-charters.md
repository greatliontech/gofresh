train (user ruling 2026-08-26): forcing a when-needed capability into
an execution order is fake scheduling. Each entry is an issue doc in
its owning repo carrying its full charter; this register is the
cross-repo index. An entry activates when its condition fires — at
activation it charters a numbered train chunk (or its own plan) and
leaves this register.
