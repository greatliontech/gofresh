  - Qualifies non-directive single-definition package-scope `*NamedStruct` variables (`!claimedVars[v]`, where `ptr, ok := types.Unalias(v.Type()).(*types.Pointer)` and `types.Unalias(ptr.Elem())` is directly a non-empty non-standard module package-scope `*types.Named` struct via `isNonStdModuleStruct` — rejecting `**NamedStruct` and standard-library structs).

    - `protoVerifiedVars`: attested (`SingleSubject` only, recorded in `AttestationDischarges`, enters `NonVouchDischarged` iff `SingleSubject`).

    - `onceMemoDischargedVars` (kept distinct from `memoDischargedVars` / `Fact.MemoDischarges`): attestation-free (`auditedSourceOut`, enters `NonVouchDischarged` unconditionally), gated on `alias.DirectMethodsOnly && !effectiveNonMethodUsed[key]` with backward `effectiveNonMethodUsed` closure from non-method or evicted alias uses.

- **Delete `GrpcServiceVars` (`internal/dynstate/protoaudit/grpc.go`) -> `syntaxutil.StaticCompositeGlobalFact{Key, GuardTypes}`**:
  - Verifies non-directive single-definition package-scope globals (`!claimedVars[v]`) of type `RootNamed` (`RootNamed{...}`) or `*RootNamed` (`&RootNamed{...}`) over a closed **non-standard** (`!standardPkg[g.Pkg]`) **methodless** (`types.NewMethodSet(types.NewPointer(named)).Len() == 0`) named-struct schema (checking every field `0..NumFields()-1` of `RootNamed` and every reachable nested/element struct, including omitted zero fields and empty slice/array literals, and recording sorted non-empty `GuardTypes`):
    - Pure scalars (`*types.Basic` excluding `UnsafePointer` and `Uintptr`, initialized by compile-time constants);
    - By-value non-empty non-standard methodless package-scope named structs (initialized by nested `CompositeLit`s);
    - Slices/arrays of by-value non-empty non-standard methodless package-scope named structs `NamedElem` (`nil`/typed-`nil` or `CompositeLit` of `NamedElem` `CompositeLit`s);
    - Non-generic `*types.Signature` fields (including named function types such as `grpc.MethodHandler` / `grpc.StreamHandler`, initialized by `nil`, typed-`nil`, or bare non-generic top-level `*types.Func`s optionally wrapped in chains of non-generic signature-to-signature conversions);
    - `*types.Interface` fields (initialized by `nil`, compile-time scalar constants, or `unsafe.Pointer`-free typed-`nil` conversions `(*FooServer)(nil)`).
  - Enforces intra-package root/subplace use confinement (allowing whole-root `&v` only on by-value `RootNamed`, forbidding `&v` on `*RootNamed`, forbidding subplace writes, mutating builtins, slicing `v.Methods[:0]`, non-by-value-struct `&subplace`, and non-scalar subplace call arguments) + binary-wide `!directVarWritten[key]` and `!mutatedStructs[g]` across all `GuardTypes`.
  - Guard `TypeRefOfStructFull` in `freshstruct/mutated_types.go` with `!isPtr` so `&x.ptrField` / `&x[i]` on `[]*SubStruct` (`**SubStruct`) cannot bypass `MutatedStructTypes`.
