### Pillar 2: Architecture & Consolidation Opportunities

1. **Eliminate the Split-Brain Dual API on `Snapshot` vs `RuntimeBinding`/`Unit`**:
   - Currently, `gofresh` maintains two complete lifecycle APIs in parallel:
     - **Generation 1 (`view.go` on `*Snapshot`)**: `Capture`, `CaptureBatch`, `CaptureObserved`, `CaptureObservedBatch`, `AttachObservation`, `Check`, `CheckBatch`, `CheckObserved`, `CheckObservedBatch`, `Sibling`, `Validate` — used in production **only by `gomutant`**.

2. **Unify `Unit` (`ProducingUnit`) and `PackageUnit` (`unit.go`)**:
   - In `unit.go` and `evidence.md` (`REQ-fresh-built-unit` vs `REQ-fresh-package-unit`), `Unit` and `PackageUnit` are two parallel exported types with identical lifecycle methods (`Register`, `Submit`, `Abort`, `Close`, `Execute`, `Account`), differing only in whether they finalize 1 subject (`Finalized`) or $N$ subjects (`FinalizedProcess`). A single unit type (or thin 1-subject projection) eliminates half of `unit.go` and merges `REQ-fresh-built-unit` with `REQ-fresh-package-unit`.

3. **Collapse the 3-Way Duplication of Toolchain Dependency Specs & Consolidate the 6 Memo Specs in `closure.md`**:
   - **Toolchain Content Identity (`gofresh/toolchain-content@1`)** is specified three times across `overview.md` (`REQ-fresh-toolchain-dependencies`, 6 clauses), `closure.md` (`REQ-closure-observability-toolchain-key`, 9 clauses), and `evidence.md` (`REQ-fresh-dependency-identities`, 6 clauses).

4. **Replace Ad-Hoc Library/Pattern Carve-Outs in `closure.md` with Unified Algebraic Rules**:
   - `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-*` (19 requirements, ~900 lines of spec) and `REQ-closure-observability-*` (20 requirements, ~800 lines of spec) have accreted named library exceptions (`golang.org/x/sys/unix.mapper`, `gopkg.in/yaml.v3.structMap`, `pgregory.net/rapid`, protobuf descriptor globals) and syntactic micro-patterns (`mu.Lock(); defer mu.Unlock(); slot.Store(&x); defer slot.Store(nil)`). Keeping their specs clearly separated (or modularized) prevents fleet CLI/OS utilities from muddying the core freshness domain contract.
