# Widened-Subject `progSummary` Memo Disabling, `contextCancelMethodFP` Memo Resets & Tuple-Agnostic Callee Screening

- **Status:** Open
- **Governing contract:** [`docs/specs/closure.md`](../specs/closure.md) (`REQ-closure-observability-toolchain-key`, `REQ-closure-observability-batch-equivalence`, `REQ-fresh-dependency-identities`), [`docs/specs/guards.md`](../specs/guards.md)
- **Lands:** Chunk 1 in [`docs/plans/plan.md`](../plans/plan.md)

## Problem

Three interacting closure-walk mechanisms in `internal/closure` defeat whole-program and per-subject dynamic-value memoization when analyzing packages with widened subjects and `context.Context` call chains (causing `ComputeObservabilityBatch` to take `6m04s` across 273 test subjects on a 462-package SSA program, with `685,000+` unclosed invoke evaluations and `500,000+` callee rejections):

1. **`reach.enumeratedRoot == true` Disables `inCycleScope` and `inProgScope` for the Entire Subject Walk**
   - In `newFreshParamAnalysis` ([`internal/closure/freshparam.go`](../../internal/closure/freshparam.go) lines 494–501), whenever `reach.enumeratedRoot` is `true` (a subject widens to the maximal closure), `fp.enumRoot` is set to `reach.subjectRoot` (`!= nil`) while `fp.enumOnly` remains `false`.
   - In [`internal/closure/dynamic_walk.go`](../../internal/closure/dynamic_walk.go) lines 249–250 and [`internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go`](../../internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go) lines 119–121:
     ```go
     inCycleScope := (fp.enumOnly || fp.enumRoot == nil) && (fp.inProgramSites || fp.inFieldStore || fp.enumOnly) && value.Parent() != fp.enumRoot && value.Parent() != fp.subjectRoot
     inProgScope := outsideCallee && inCycleScope && !fp.ignoreContextValuePayload && fp.enumRecvNamed == nil
     ```
     Because `fp.enumOnly` is `false` and `fp.enumRoot != nil`, `(fp.enumOnly || fp.enumRoot == nil)` evaluates to `false` for 100% of widened subjects.
   - All eight whole-program memo tables on `progSummary` (`progClosedDone`, `progCarrierDone`, `progInvokeTargets`, `progInvokeFailed`, `progTypesMemo`, `progTypesFailed`, `progFuncsMemo`, `progFuncsFailed`) are completely bypassed, forcing every widened subject in a batch to re-walk the entire transitive SSA program from scratch.

2. **`contextCancelMethodFP` Calls `resetDynamicMemos()` Before Checking Site Memos and Discards Work on Return**
   - In `closedInvokeTargets` ([`internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go`](../../internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go) line 118), `fp = contextCancelMethodFP(site, fp)` runs before consulting `pFailed`/`pTargets` or `fp.invokeFailed`/`fp.invokeTargets`.
   - `withIgnoreContextValuePayload` ([`internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go`](../../internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go) lines 952–974, also called from [`internal/closure/callee_return.go`](../../internal/closure/callee_return.go) lines 66 and 146) copies `fp`, sets `ignoreContextValuePayload = true` (forcing `inProgScope = false`), and calls `copied.resetDynamicMemos()`, allocating empty maps on every `(context.Context).Err`, `Done`, and `Deadline` invoke site.
   - Entries written to `fp.invokeTargets`/`fp.invokeFailed` and `fp.closedDone`/`fp.carrierDone` during the receiver walk live only on the local `copied` struct and are discarded when `closedInvokeTargets` returns, causing every `ctx.Err()` site within the same function and across subjects to re-walk the entire `context.Context` provenance chain.

3. **`paramMaySupplyDynamicSeen` and `calleeParamMayAffectResult` Ignore `tupleIndex` on Multi-Return Callees**
   - When evaluating `tupleIndex == 0` (`context.Context`) of a multi-return method such as `(*Session).beginOperation(ctx context.Context) (context.Context, func(*error))`, `paramMaySupplyDynamicSeen` ([`internal/closure/attribution_harness.go`](../../internal/closure/attribution_harness.go) lines 279–348) and `calleeParamMayAffectResult` ([`internal/closure/callee_return.go`](../../internal/closure/callee_return.go) lines 288–303, 616–721) inspect all referrers of `callee.Params[0]` (`s *Session`) without considering which tuple index is being queried.
   - Because `s` is captured by a `*ssa.MakeClosure` whose sole referrer inside `beginOperation` is `*ssa.Return` at `Results[1]` (never `Results[0]` and never invoked inside `beginOperation`), `paramMaySupplyDynamicSeen` returns `true` for `tupleIndex == 0` and requires `struct_arg_walk` on `s *Session`, rejecting `beginOperation` (`55,446` times) and cascading into unclosed `(context.Context).Err` dispatches across callers.

## Soundness Constraints & Resolution Scope

1. **Dynamic-Walk Memo Table & Scope-Predicate Consolidation**:
   - Extract `dynamicMemoTables` (`closedDone`, `carrierDone`, `typesMemo`, `typesFailed`, `funcsMemo`, `funcsFailed`, `invokeTargets`, `invokeFailed`) and mode-indexed `[2]dynamicMemoTables` (`memoPair *[2]dynamicMemoTables` on `subjectQuery`, `progMemos, enumMemos [2]dynamicMemoTables` on `callerReferenceSummary`, selected via `fp.subjMemo()` / `fp.progMemo()` indexed by `fp.ignoreContextValuePayload`), collapsing the 10 `if fp.enumOnly` dispatch/walk branches in [`internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go`](../../internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go) and [`internal/closure/dynamic_walk.go`](../../internal/closure/dynamic_walk.go) and automatically retaining `[1]` memos across repeated `withIgnoreContextValuePayload` (`(context.Context).Err`, `Done`, `Deadline`) transitions without cross-mode contamination.
   - Centralize `fp.canReadProgMemo()` and `fp.canWriteProgMemo()` on `*freshParamAnalysis` (replacing the 4 divergent open-coded `inProgScope`/`inCycleScope` predicates in `dispatch_targets.go` lines 119–120 and `dynamic_walk.go` lines 129–130, 248–250, 1378) and extract `(s *callerReferenceSummary) ssaIndex() ssaProgramIndex` (replacing the 4 duplicated 12-field struct literals in `freshparam.go` and `attribution_provenance.go`).
2. **Widened-Subject `progSummary` Sharing (`REQ-closure-observability-batch-equivalence`)**:
   - Allow widened subjects in whole-program scope (`fp.inProgramSites || fp.inFieldStore`) outside `fp.enumRoot` and `fp.subjectRoot` to read and write `summary.progMemos` **only when every positive and negative consultation of per-subject state marks `*fp.usedSubject = true`**.
   - Specifically, audit and instrument `paramCrossingEligible` ([`internal/closure/freshparam.go`](../../internal/closure/freshparam.go) lines 636–645, which consults per-subject `fp.dynamic[fn]` and `fp.functions[fn]`), `attributedFreeVarBindings` ([`internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go`](../../internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go) line 753, which consults `fp.functions[mc.Parent()]`), `dynamic_walk.go` lines 1579–1597 (`fp.enumRoot != nil && ... && len(fp.enumFieldStores) > 0`), and every read of `fp.callers`, `fp.enumRoot`, `fp.enumSites`, `fp.enumFieldStores`, and `fp.enumRecvNamed` so neither a subject-specific acceptance nor a subject-specific refusal can leak into `summary.progMemos`.
   - Enforce `fp.enumRecvNamed == nil` in `fp.canReadProgMemo()` (because `userNamed == fp.enumRecvNamed` at [`internal/closure/dynamic_walk.go`](../../internal/closure/dynamic_walk.go) lines 1471–1479 is an optimistic subject-specific exemption) and require `len(fp.enumStructVisiting) == 0 && fp.baseCarrierNamed == nil` before trusting negative (`*Failed` / `!closedDone`) `progMemos` entries (`dynamic_walk.go` line 423) so negative entries populated without optimistic exemptions never falsely reject closed receiver or struct cycles.
3. **Shared Tuple-Indexed Callee Parameter Screening**: Extract shared `returnExcludesTupleIndex` and closure-capture confinement helpers across `paramMaySupplyDynamicSeen` ([`internal/closure/attribution_harness.go`](../../internal/closure/attribution_harness.go) lines 279–348) and `calleeParamMayAffectResult` ([`internal/closure/callee_return.go`](../../internal/closure/callee_return.go) lines 616–774) so a `*ssa.MakeClosure` whose only use in the callee is returning at `ret.Results[j]` (`j != tupleIndex`) does not mark captured parameters as supplying `tupleIndex`.
