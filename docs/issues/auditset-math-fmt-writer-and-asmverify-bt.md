- **Status:** Open
- **Governing contract:** [`docs/specs/closure.md`](../specs/closure.md) (`REQ-closure-coverage`, `REQ-closure-view-maximal`, `REQ-closure-identity-strategy`, `REQ-closure-observability-toolchain-key`, `REQ-closure-observability-audited-set`, `REQ-closure-observability-entropy`, `REQ-closure-observability-writer-sink`, `REQ-closure-observability-dispatch`, `REQ-closure-blindspot`), [`docs/specs/evidence.md`](../specs/evidence.md) (`REQ-fresh-dependency-identities`), [`docs/specs/overview.md`](../specs/overview.md) (`REQ-fresh-commit-independent`), [`docs/specs/guards.md`](../specs/guards.md) (`REQ-guard-runtime-relevance`, `REQ-guard-selective-capture`), [`docs/specs/purity.md`](../specs/purity.md)
- **Lands:** Chunks 8, 12 in [`docs/plans/plan.md`](../plans/plan.md)

## Problem

1. **`collect.sinkOnly` Pollution Blocking `fmt.Fprint*` Delegating Writers, `fmt.Fscan*` Pure Readers, Local Closures, `go/parser` AST Nodes & `-buildvcs` `ReadBuildInfo` Gating**
   - `inMemoryFormattedSink` ([`internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go`](../../internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go)) passes `sinkOnly: true` into `walkDynamicValueUncached` (`15` special-case branches in [`dynamic_walk.go`](../../internal/closure/dynamic_walk.go)), disabling `*ssa.Alloc`, `*ssa.Call`, and `*ssa.FreeVar` and blocking `sha256.New()` / `hash.Hash`, pure delegating wrappers (`*text/tabwriter.Writer` over in-memory sinks), symmetric `fmt.Fscan*` pure readers (`*strings.Reader`, `*bytes.Reader`, `*bytes.Buffer`), non-escaping local helper closures taking `func()` parameters (`MakeClosure` call sites in [`freshparam.go`](../../internal/closure/freshparam.go)), and sealed `go/ast` node interface dispatches returned by `go/parser`, while `runtime/debug.ReadBuildInfo` awaits `EffectiveBuildVCS` and `go.mod` metadata gating before admission in [`internal/auditset`](../../internal/auditset) and `internal/closure`.

2. **Non-Standard Assembly Verifier (`internal/closure/internal/asmverify`) Rejects `BT*` Bit Tests, Carrier-Free Go Lookup Tables, CPU-Feature `(SB)` Globals & Unreached `.s` Files in Pure-Go `init()`**
   - `isAsmReadOnlyCompareMnemonic` ([`isa.go`](../../internal/closure/internal/asmverify/isa.go)) omits read-only x86 bit-test mnemonics `BT`, `BTW`, `BTL`, and `BTQ`.
   - `parseAsmLocalSBRef` ([`parse.go`](../../internal/closure/internal/asmverify/parse.go)) rejects `LEA*`/`MOV* $sym(SB)` reads of unmutated carrier-free Go lookup tables (`[256]byte`) and cross-package `init()`-populated CPU-feature structs (`cpu·X86+0(SB)`).
   - `addReachedPackageFiles` ([`analyzer.go`](../../internal/closure/analyzer.go)) poisons `startupSummary` on pure-Go `init()` functions whenever any unreached `TEXT` in a `PackageSafe` `.s` package is unverified.

## Soundness Constraints & Resolution Scope

1. **Chunk 8 — `asmverify` `BT*`, Shared `buildAsmBlockCFG`, `(SB)` Lookup/CPU Globals & `PackageSafe` / Per-`TEXT` `Verified` Split**:
   - Add exact `BT`/`BTW`/`BTL`/`BTQ` to `isAsmReadOnlyCompareMnemonic`, extract shared preprocessor-aware `buildAsmBlockCFG` (`cfg.go`, rejecting fall-off at `TEXT` end) + GPR taint fixpoint (`R14` + `LEA*`/`MOV* $sym(SB)` lookup-table store-freedom), discharge `ExternalSBRef` via dedicated SSA carrier-free Go lookup-table immutability (never `dynstate`) and `init()`-populated CPU-feature global immutability (Tier-2 static CPU-feature queries, refused in Tier-1 `Maximal`), and split `VerifiedPackage` into `PackageSafe` + transitive per-`TEXT` `Verified` + `AllTextsVerified` (while retaining `math`'s 30 constants + 31 universally `internal/cpu`-free IEEE-754 exact functions in Tier-1 `auditset` and excluding compiler-intrinsic `FMA` and runtime `useFMA`/`hasVX` operations at both tiers per `REQ-guard-runtime-relevance`).
2. **Chunk 12 — `sinkOnly` Deletion, Parameterized `inMemoryStreamOperand`, Local `MakeClosure` Propagation, `go/parser` AST Nodes & `-buildvcs` Gate**:
   - Update `REQ-closure-observability-writer-sink`, `REQ-closure-observability-audited-set`, `REQ-closure-observability-dispatch`, `REQ-closure-coverage`, and `REQ-closure-view-maximal` in `docs/specs/closure.md`, delete `collect.sinkOnly`, implement `inMemoryStreamOperand` (`stream_sinks.go`) shared across `fmt.Fprint*` writers (`*bytes.Buffer`, `*strings.Builder`, `io.Discard`, `hash.Hash`, confined `*tabwriter.Writer`/`*bufio.Writer`), `fmt.Fscan*` pure readers (`*strings.Reader`, `*bytes.Reader`, `*bytes.Buffer`, confined `*bufio.Reader`), and `go/parser` (`ParseExpr` + `src != nil` `ParseFile`/`ParseExprFrom` + sealed `go/ast` interfaces), propagate closed inline function arguments across non-escaping local `MakeClosure` call sites in `freshparam.go`, wire `buildflags.EffectiveBuildVCS` + `go.mod` metadata gating `runtime/debug.ReadBuildInfo` across Tier 1 and Tier 2, unify `closure`'s callee admission ladders, bump `ObservationRTA` and `effectScanStrategy`, and retire this issue.
