## Problem

1. **Fragmented Stdlib Purity Tables & Brittle `stmts[0..k]` AST Templates Across `internal/auditset`, `toolchain`, and `dynstate`**
   - [`internal/closure/internal/toolchain/contracts_pure.go`](../../internal/closure/internal/toolchain/contracts_pure.go) hand-maintains 6 selector maps (`AllowedPure{Fmt,Math,MathRand,Time,Sync,SyncAtomic,Reflect}Selectors`) duplicating [`internal/auditset`](../../internal/auditset), while `dynstate` (`auditedValuePlane`, `isPureDeterministicConeStdlibCall`, `isPureMemoCall`) and `propharness` maintain separate stdlib purity tables and format-verb parsers.
   - `internal/closure/internal/toolchain/*.go` (`16,698` LOC) relies on hardcoded `stmts[0..k]` AST templates (`audit_testdeps.go`, `audit_testlog.go`, `audit_bufio.go`, `audit_testing_*.go`, `audit_os_*.go`), `os`-only inactive-branch discovery (`2,292` LOC), and a `1,863`-LOC `testing`-specific custom writer verifier (`audit_testing_writer.go`) that fails on `godst` `testdeps` dead-branch (`!dst`) and runtime-linknamed fd-write (`-tags=dst`) shapes.
   - Deterministic `math` functions (`math.Inf`, `math.NaN`, `math.IsNaN`, `math.IsInf`, `math.Abs`, `math.Sqrt`, `math.Log`, `math.Float64bits`, etc.) and `runtime/debug.ReadBuildInfo` are not yet mechanically audited.

2. **`collect.sinkOnly` Pollution Blocking `fmt.Fprint*` Delegating Writers, `fmt.Fscan*` Pure Readers, Local Closures & `go/parser` AST Nodes**
   - `inMemoryFormattedSink` ([`internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go`](../../internal/closure/dispatch_targets.go)) passes `sinkOnly: true` into `walkDynamicValueUncached` (`15` special-case branches in [`dynamic_walk.go`](../../internal/closure/dynamic_walk.go)), disabling `*ssa.Alloc`, `*ssa.Call`, and `*ssa.FreeVar` and blocking `sha256.New()` / `hash.Hash`, pure delegating wrappers (`*text/tabwriter.Writer` over in-memory sinks), symmetric `fmt.Fscan*` pure readers (`*strings.Reader`, `*bytes.Reader`, `*bytes.Buffer`), non-escaping local helper closures taking `func()` parameters (`MakeClosure` call sites in `freshparam.go`), and sealed `go/ast` node interface dispatches returned by `go/parser`.

3. **Non-Standard Assembly Verifier (`internal/closure/internal/asmverify`) Rejects `BT*` Bit Tests, Carrier-Free Go Lookup Tables, CPU-Feature `(SB)` Globals & Unreached `.s` Files in Pure-Go `init()`**
   - `isAsmReadOnlyCompareMnemonic` ([`isa.go`](../../internal/closure/internal/asmverify/isa.go)) omits read-only x86 bit-test mnemonics `BT`, `BTW`, `BTL`, and `BTQ`.
   - `parseAsmLocalSBRef` ([`parse.go`](../../internal/closure/internal/asmverify/parse.go)) rejects `LEA*`/`MOV* $sym(SB)` reads of unmutated carrier-free Go lookup tables (`[256]byte`) and cross-package `init()`-populated CPU-feature structs (`cpu·X86+0(SB)`).
   - `addReachedPackageFiles` ([`analyzer.go`](../../internal/closure/analyzer.go)) poisons `startupSummary` on pure-Go `init()` functions whenever any unreached `TEXT` in a `PackageSafe` `.s` package is unverified.

## Soundness Constraints & Resolution Scope

1. **Chunk 7 — Unified `auditset` + 4-Layer Semantic `toolchain` Kernel & Pure `math` / `Chdir` / `ReadBuildInfo` Verifiers**:
   - Export unified stdlib surface and deterministic value-computation queries in `internal/auditset`, deriving `contracts_pure.go` selector maps and replacing `dynstate`'s `auditedValuePlane`.
   - Build the 4-layer syntax-level semantic `toolchain` kernel (`kernel_scope.go`, `kernel_inactive.go`, `kernel_cfg.go`, `sink_verify.go` — zero Go-version checks, zero `dst*` names), deleting `audit_testing_writer.go` and verifying vanilla Go and `godst` (plain and `-tags=dst`) from one byte-sink + retry + sticky-error-latch contract.
   - Verify `(*os.File).Chdir`, `testing`'s `Chdir` harness methods, `runtime/debug` (`ReadBuildInfo` AST verifier only — keeping `ReadBuildInfo` un-admitted in `auditset`/`closure` until Chunk 12 wires `EffectiveBuildVCS` and `go.mod` metadata so `REQ-fresh-commit-independent` holds at every commit), and `math`: per `REQ-guard-runtime-relevance` (`guards.md:158-167`, where `CodeResult` omits `Machine`) and `REQ-closure-observability-audited-set` (`closure.md:1741-1751`), admit the 31 constants + 32 universally `internal/cpu`-free IEEE-754 exact functions in Tier-1 `auditset`, and admit selected-`(GOOS, GOARCH)` `internal/cpu`-free `math` functions in Tier-2 `Observability` while refusing runtime `internal/cpu`-branching approximations (`useFMA` on `amd64`, `hasVX` on `s390x`).
2. **Chunk 8 — `asmverify` `BT*`, Shared `buildAsmBlockCFG`, `(SB)` Lookup/CPU Globals & `PackageSafe` / Per-`TEXT` `Verified` Split**:
   - Add exact `BT`/`BTW`/`BTL`/`BTQ` to `isAsmReadOnlyCompareMnemonic`, extract shared preprocessor-aware `buildAsmBlockCFG` (`cfg.go`, rejecting fall-off at `TEXT` end) + GPR taint fixpoint (`R14` + `LEA*`/`MOV* $sym(SB)` lookup-table store-freedom), discharge `ExternalSBRef` via dedicated SSA carrier-free Go lookup-table immutability (never `dynstate`) and `init()`-populated CPU-feature global immutability (Tier-2 static CPU-feature queries, refused in Tier-1 `Maximal`), and split `VerifiedPackage` into `PackageSafe` + transitive per-`TEXT` `Verified` + `AllTextsVerified`.
3. **Chunk 12 — `sinkOnly` Deletion, Parameterized `inMemoryStreamOperand`, Local `MakeClosure` Propagation, `go/parser` AST Nodes & `-buildvcs` Gate**:
   - Update `REQ-closure-observability-writer-sink`, `REQ-closure-observability-audited-set`, `REQ-closure-observability-dispatch`, `REQ-closure-coverage`, and `REQ-closure-view-maximal` in `docs/specs/closure.md`, delete `collect.sinkOnly`, implement `inMemoryStreamOperand` (`stream_sinks.go`) shared across `fmt.Fprint*` writers (`*bytes.Buffer`, `*strings.Builder`, `io.Discard`, `hash.Hash`, confined `*tabwriter.Writer`/`*bufio.Writer`), `fmt.Fscan*` pure readers (`*strings.Reader`, `*bytes.Reader`, `*bytes.Buffer`, confined `*bufio.Reader`), and `go/parser` (`ParseExpr` + `src != nil` `ParseFile`/`ParseExprFrom` + sealed `go/ast` interfaces), propagate closed inline function arguments across non-escaping local `MakeClosure` call sites in `freshparam.go`, wire `buildflags.EffectiveBuildVCS` + `go.mod` metadata gating `runtime/debug.ReadBuildInfo` across Tier 1 and Tier 2, unify `closure`'s callee admission ladders, bump `ObservationRTA` and `effectScanStrategy`, and retire this issue.
