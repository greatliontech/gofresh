## Problem

1. **Six Parallel Raw-`go/ast` Intra-Procedural Walkers Duplicating Go's Surface Syntax**
   - `dynstate.FactOf` derives modular per-package summaries before downstream importers or generic type instantiations are known, including uninstantiated generic functions (`func Must[T any](...)`, whose bodies `go/ssa` leaves empty with `Blocks == nil`).
   - Operating directly over raw `go/ast` across `boundValueJudged` and `receiverReadOnlyMethods` (plus separate `onceBodyFillsReceiver` and inline `onceExemptions` verifiers) in [`internal/dynstate/summary_leakfree.go`](../../internal/dynstate/summary_leakfree.go) (`1,486` lines), `enclosingConfinesRoots` and `returnEnvFreeFunctions` in [`internal/dynstate/summary_env.go`](../../internal/dynstate/summary_env.go) (`1,977` lines), and `recordDynamicGlobalUses` / `walkSyncVarCalls` in [`internal/dynstate/summary_uses.go`](../../internal/dynstate/summary_uses.go) (`2,010` lines) forces every walker to re-decode Go's ~25 lvalue, aliasing, assignment, call, composite-literal, range, type-switch, builtin, method-value, and closure-capture forms independently.
   - That duplication causes concrete maintenance amplification and surface-form soundness/precision divergences across:
     - Go 1.26+ `new(expr)` value-initializing pointers vs. `new(T)` zero allocations (`TypesInfo.Types[arg].IsType()`),
     - `TypeSwitchStmt` implicit case-clause `*types.Var` bindings (`TypesInfo.Implicits[*ast.CaseClause]`),
     - Addressed base-expression chains (`&((*ptr).scalar)`, `(*arrPtr)[:]`, `&getSlice()[0]`) and auto-addressed value-type `MethodVal` selectors,
     - Map keys carrying signatures (`TypeCarriesSignature` checking `u.Key()`, `LHSMapWriteKey` on `AssignStmt`/`IncDecStmt`/`RangeStmt`, and pointer-unwrapped elided-`&` composite literals),
     - By-value `sync.Once`/`sync.Cond` copies (`TypeReachesSyncNoCopyByValue`, struct `==`/`!=`, and narrowing `AuditedSynchronization` away from `Cond.Wait` and persistent-flag `Once.Do`),
     - Co-argument disjointness on single-call constructor/insertion exemptions (`makeBundle(buf, buf)`), `FuncAuditTarget` propagation on plain call arguments to insertion helpers, `DeferralResolution.IsObjectClosed` + `ActiveCarrierLinkGraph` + complete `recordOpaqueDynamicVars`/conversion `CarrierLinks`, Tarjan cycle-depth memo gating on recursive proofs, and per-variable lexical scope checks (`lhsObj.Pos() < body.Pos() || lhsObj.Pos() > body.End()`) across nested closures.

2. **Multi-Concern `2,000`-Line Summary Files in `internal/dynstate` (`summary_uses.go`, `summary_env.go`)**
   - [`internal/dynstate/summary_uses.go`](../../internal/dynstate/summary_uses.go) (`2,010` lines) combines general dynamic-global use tracking (`recordDynamicGlobalUses`) with `sync.Map`/`sync.Pool`/function-reference-region/opaque-var verifiers (`walkSyncVarCalls`, `dataMemoVars`, `provenSharedPools`, `recordFunctionReferenceRegions`, `recordOpaqueDynamicVars`).
   - [`internal/dynstate/summary_env.go`](../../internal/dynstate/summary_env.go) (`1,977` lines) combines store-plane environment-free function-literal and field-registrant classification with return-plane audited value and return-field registrant derivation.

## Soundness Constraints & Resolution Scope

1. **Canonical Per-Body Lowering Kernel (`internal/dynstate/flowir`)**:
   - Build `internal/dynstate/flowir` (`LowerBody` / `LowerFunc` over `ast.Node` + `*types.Info` + `*types.Pkg`, handling both concrete and uninstantiated `*types.TypeParam` generic bodies uniformly) to desugar Go's surface syntax **once** into normalized places (`LocalVar`, `ParamVar`, `RecvVar`, `PkgGlobal`, `OuterVar`, `FieldProj`, `IndexProj`, `DerefProj`, `TransientRoot`) and canonical value/memory/effect operations (`Assign`, `AddrOf` covering `&place`, array `[:]`, and auto-addressed `MethodVal`s, `AllocFresh` distinguishing `new(T)` zero allocation via `IsType()` from Go 1.26+ `new(expr)` allocate+store and unwrapping elided-`&` composite-literal element types, `Copy`, `Append`, `MapWrite`, `ChanSend`, `ChanRecv`, `CallPlain`, `CallMethod`, `CallIndirect`, `OnceDo`, `MakeClosure` with per-variable lexical outer-root classification, `SpawnGo`, `Defer`, `Return`, `TypeSwitchBind`, and value-comparison/conversion reads).
   - Enforce `IsBuiltinNewType` in `syntaxutil` and `internal/dynstate/internal/freshstruct` as well so Go 1.26+ `new(expr)` never masquerades as a zero allocation.
2. **Unified Confinement/Freshness Summaries, Spec Alignment & Soundness Hardening on `flowir`**:
   - Update `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-receiver-proof`, `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-audited-discharges`, `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-init-aliasing`, `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-leak-free`, and `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-registration-audit` in [`docs/specs/closure.md`](../specs/closure.md) (and re-pin `.stipulator/bindings/closure.textproto`) to reflect confinement synchronization narrowing (`Mutex`/`RWMutex` + `Cond.{Signal,Broadcast}` on uncopied receivers vs. `Cond.Wait` / persistent-flag `Once.Do`) and the normalized aliasing/spine/carrier invariants.
   - Express parameter/local leak-free & retention-free confinement (`boundValueJudged`), pointer-receiver read-only & retention-free confinement (`receiverReadOnlyMethods`), shared `sync.Once.Do` receiver-fill verification, closure root confinement (`enclosingConfinesRoots`), and local return-freshness (`returnEnvFreeFunctions`) over `flowir`, enforcing all surface and type invariants above (`TypeReachesSyncNoCopyByValue`, map-key signature carriers, addressed lvalue base chains, co-argument disjointness, `FuncAuditTarget` plain-call propagation, Tarjan cycle-depth memo gating, `IsObjectClosed` + `ActiveCarrierLinkGraph`, and complete `recordOpaqueDynamicVars` / conversion `CarrierLinks`) and bumping `DynamicStateStrategy`.
3. **Summary File Splits, Surface Inventory Guard & Direct Kernel/Property Tests**:
   - Split `summary_uses.go` (`summary_uses.go` + `summary_uses_sync_opaque.go`) and `summary_env.go` (`summary_env_stores.go` + `summary_env_returns.go`) within `package dynstate`, register `flowir` in `TestExportedSurfaceInventory` ([`surface_test.go`](../../surface_test.go)) and align the leaf-package count comment in `.stipulator/policy.textproto`, preserve all stipulator-bound witness test names in `internal/dynstate/dynstate_test.go`, and add direct unit/property tests in `internal/dynstate/flowir/` and adversarial surface-form regression tests in `internal/dynstate/`.
