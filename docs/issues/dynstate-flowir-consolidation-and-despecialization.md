## Problem

1. **Redundant 23-Field `String*` Shadow Lattice on `dynstate.Fact` vs. Whole-System `closure` `unsafe` Boundary (`~3,450` LOC)**
   - `dynstate.Fact` currently carries 23 `String*` shadow fields (`HasStringHeaderUnsafe`, `StringEscapes`, `StringAttributedUses`, `StringEnvCarrying`, `StringEnvCallable`, `StringEnvCarryingFns`, `StringEnvCall*`, `StringCarrierLinks`, `StringReturnEnv*`, `StringParamInsertion*`, `StringSink*`, `StringCallbackTargets`), a duplicate `allowString=true` environment pass in `facts_derive.go`, `~1,600` LOC of string-unsafe call-graph/container tracking in `flow_cache.go`, and `~575` LOC in `summary_uses.go` — even though `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-carrier-free` (`closure.md:400-414`) and `REQ-closure-observability-unsafe` (`closure.md:1702-1728`) assign non-type-safe memory write channels to `closure` (`MaximalClosure` + `Observability`), and `SharedDynamicStateCause` (`judgment.go:49-56`) requires a dynamic-carrying variable `Culprit`.
   - Meanwhile, `internal/closure/observability.go:1177` carves `unsafe.Pointer` out of `packageWideMaximalHazard` unconditionally even under `PackageExecution` / `GeneralExecution`, allowing an unreached sibling test in the shared process to mutate a carrier-free `var SharedBanner string` via `(**[5]byte)(unsafe.Pointer(&SharedBanner))` while `maximal.go` and `observability.go` over-conservatively block compile-time layout intrinsics `unsafe.Sizeof`, `Alignof`, and `Offsetof`.
   - Sixteen exported helpers across `internal/dynstate/syntaxutil/` (`ast.go`, `keys.go`, `types.go`) also have zero external callers.

2. **Remaining Duplicate Walkers Across `refconfine`, Intra-Procedural `dynstate` Summaries, Cone Resolution (`Compose` / `Explain`), and `propharness`**
   - [`refconfine/confine.go`](../../internal/dynstate/refconfine/confine.go) (`1,727` LOC) still walks raw `go/ast` with 8 manual tuple-unpacking and 3 variadic-mapping blocks instead of consuming cached `flowir.Body`s + `BuildScopedMemAliases`.
   - `summary_leakfree.go`, `summary_env_stores.go`, `summary_env_returns.go`, and `summary_uses.go` maintain 4 overlapping root-confinement walkers, 2 `(freshObj, elemOwned)` fresh-allocation GFPs, separate `returnEnvFreeFunctions` / `paramInsertionFreeParams` passes, and 4 single-write local/global helpers.
   - `fixpoint_compose.go`, `fixpoint_env.go`, and `explain.go` duplicate `Fact` validation, 5 cone traversals, 5 variant-intersection loops, `OwnerUnproven` derivation (`220` LOC), and run the joint LFP three times per `ExplainDynamicState` call.
   - [`discharges_once_memo.go`](../../internal/dynstate/discharges_once_memo.go) maintains two divergent raw-AST cone walkers (`verifyOnceMemoComputeCone` and `verifyOnceMemoSiblingMethod`), [`discharges_memo.go`](../../internal/dynstate/discharges_memo.go) (`819` LOC) matches rigid `stmts[0..4]` AST indices, and [`internal/propharness`](../../internal/propharness) duplicates `refconfine.PkgIndex`, settled-var/memo checks, `auditset` stdlib purity, and verification caching.

3. **Over-Specialized Domain Templates & Hardcoded Field/Method/Role Names (`~4,070` LOC across `protoaudit`, `discharges_memo`, and `discharges_mapping`)**
   - `ProtoExtAliases` ([`protoaudit/clusters.go`](../../internal/dynstate/protoaudit/clusters.go)) restricts read-only package-variable alias inheritance to cross-package `*NamedStruct` variables targeting `protoVerifiedVars` under `--single-subject`.
   - `GrpcServiceVars` ([`protoaudit/grpc.go`](../../internal/dynstate/protoaudit/grpc.go), `277` LOC) hardcodes gRPC field names and gates static composite tables on `--single-subject` even when binary-wide `DirectVarWrites` and `MutatedStructTypes` prove zero mutation across all execution modes.
   - `ProtoRuntimeTypes` and `ProtoClusters` (`protoaudit/*.go`, `8,248` LOC) encode 20+ hardcoded protobuf field, method, and role names, and `MappingDischarges` ([`discharges_mapping.go`](../../internal/dynstate/discharges_mapping.go)) implements bespoke raw-AST lock/field walkers.

## Soundness Constraints & Resolution Scope

1. **Chunk 6 — Whole-System `unsafe` Boundary, `String*` Deletion & `syntaxutil` Cleanup**:
   - Exempt compile-time `unsafe.Sizeof`, `Alignof`, and `Offsetof` in `closure` (`maximal.go` and `observability.go`, retaining file hashing and operand syntax type/symbol dependency walking); evaluate execution-agnostic `PackageHasUnconfinedUnsafeMemoryHazard` (`Sizeof`/`Alignof`/`Offsetof`, `refconfine`-verified read-only `StringData`/`Slice(StringData)` projections, pure scalar bitcasts, and `IsomorphicValueLayout` casts) across loaded non-std packages on `tier2Base` (coordinating `maximal.go`, `observability.go`, `startupSummary`, and persisted `map[string]Observability` memos keyed by `ObservationRTA` so `Proofs()` disk-memo sharing stays execution-agnostic), and surface `Observability.RequiresSingleSubject` bound into `ObservationProof.Evidence` (preserving `ObservationProof` wire keys under `REQ-fresh-fingerprint-record`, enforced against `SingleSubjectExecution` in `admittedObservation` / `decideAfterClosureObserved`, and recorded in `AcceptedAuthority`); delete all 23 `String*` fields and the duplicate `allowString` env pass across `dynstate` and `explain.go`; delete the zero-caller helpers in `syntaxutil`; and bump `DynamicStateStrategy`, `ObservationRTA`, `effectScanStrategy`, and `scanEntryVersion`.
2. **Chunk 9 — `refconfine` `flowir` Re-Hosting, Unified Intra-Procedural Confinement & `AnalyzeCone`**:

3. **Chunk 10 — `ReadOnlyVarAliases`, Attestation-Free `StaticCompositeGlobals`, Unified `flowir` Memoizers & `discharges_mapping` Lock Regions**:
