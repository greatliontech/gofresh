## Problem

1. **Parallel Raw-`go/ast` Walkers Alongside `flowir` (`~6,800` LOC) & Four Active Soundness/Precision Defects**
   - [`internal/dynstate/summary_uses.go`](../../internal/dynstate/summary_uses.go) (`2,004` LOC) and [`summary_uses_sync_opaque.go`](../../internal/dynstate/summary_uses_sync_opaque.go) (`629` LOC) still walk raw `go/ast` with manual parent-avoidance sets (`readContext`, `calledSelectors`, `goCalls`, `skipInteriors`, `scanSites` AST stack) and 144 lines of verbatim-duplicated map-merging boilerplate in `walkAttributedDecl`. Because `initAliasedLocals` and `markTargets` do not use `flowir.BuildScopedMemAliases` or `localCarrierAliases{root, all}`:
     1. Dereference and shared-backing-slice writes (`p := &s; *p = Carrier; escape(s)` and `a := make([]any, 1); b := a; b[0] = Carrier; escape(a)`) fail to propagate carrier aliases in `initAliasedLocals`.
     2. Writing or taking the address of an unrelated scalar field on a fresh carrier-holding struct (`box := &Box{c: Carrier}; box.n = 1`) falsely marks `Carrier` mutated/escaped.
     3. `markTargets` (`summary_uses.go` lines 92–111) and `RangeStmt` (`lines 1081–1094`) inspect the entire LHS/range target subtree and falsely mark map/slice index keys (`localMap[CarrierKey] = val`, `for _, v := range table[CarrierKey]`) as `mutated`.
   - [`discharges_once_memo.go`](../../internal/dynstate/discharges_once_memo.go) maintains copy-pasted raw-AST cone walkers in `verifyOnceMemoComputeCone` (`lines 1317–1471`) and `verifyOnceMemoSiblingMethod` (`lines 2044–2375`, raw-AST cone walker `lines 2069–2325`) that have diverged in opposite directions: `verifyOnceMemoComputeCone` omits `RangeStmt` over `*types.Chan` and `*types.Signature`, while `verifyOnceMemoSiblingMethod` omits `RangeStmt` over `*types.Map` and pointer-identity comparisons (`isPointerIdentityComparison`).
   - Nine parallel expression purity/freshness/non-smuggling classifiers across `summary_env_stores.go`, `summary_env_returns.go`, `summary_leakfree.go`, and `discharges_once_memo.go` (including `v.Raw` back-edges into `go/ast`), 5 duplicated local-taint loops in `summary_leakfree.go`, repeated per-pass `flowir.LowerFunc`/`LowerNode` lowering (4–6x per declaration), duplicated `Fact` validation and variant-intersection loops in `fixpoint_compose.go`/`fixpoint_env.go`, and 8 manual tuple-unpacking / 3 variadic-mapping blocks in [`refconfine/confine.go`](../../internal/dynstate/refconfine/confine.go) (`1,727` LOC) create ongoing maintenance and soundness-drift cost.

2. **Over-Specialized Domain Templates & Hardcoded Field/Method/Role Names (`~4,070` LOC across `protoaudit`, `discharges_memo`, and `discharges_mapping`)**
   - `ProtoExtAliases` ([`protoaudit/clusters.go`](../../internal/dynstate/protoaudit/clusters.go) lines 65–107) restricts read-only package-variable alias inheritance to cross-package `*NamedStruct` variables targeting `protoVerifiedVars` under `--single-subject`.
   - `GrpcServiceVars` ([`protoaudit/grpc.go`](../../internal/dynstate/protoaudit/grpc.go), `277` LOC) hardcodes gRPC field names (`"ServiceName"`, `"HandlerType"`, `"Methods"`, `"Streams"`, `"Metadata"`, `"MethodName"`, `"StreamName"`, `"Handler"`, `"ServerStreams"`, `"ClientStreams"`) and gates static composite tables on `--single-subject` even when binary-wide `DirectVarWrites` and `MutatedStructTypes` prove zero mutation across all execution modes.
   - `MemoDischarges` ([`discharges_memo.go`](../../internal/dynstate/discharges_memo.go), `819` LOC), `OnceMemoTypes` ([`discharges_once_memo.go`](../../internal/dynstate/discharges_once_memo.go)), and `freshstruct.FindMutexConfinedConstructorCaches` (`135` LOC) implement separate pure-memoizer engines, with `discharges_memo.go` matching rigid `stmts[0..4]` AST statement indices and maintaining a separate stdlib purity table.
   - `ProtoRuntimeTypes` and `ProtoClusters` (`protoaudit/*.go`, `8,248` LOC) encode 20+ hardcoded protobuf field, method, and role names (`"RawDescriptor"`, `"GoTypes"`, `"EnumInfos"`, `"MessageInfos"`, `"ExtensionInfos"`, `"Build"`, `"File"`, `"OneofWrappers"`, `"Exporter"`, `"GoReflectType"`, `"Desc"`, `"ExtendedType"`, `"ExtensionType"`, `"builder"`, `"desc_builder"`, `"enum"`, `"message"`, `"extension"`).
   - `MappingDischarges` ([`discharges_mapping.go`](../../internal/dynstate/discharges_mapping.go), `1,125` LOC) implements ~450 LOC of bespoke raw-AST lock/field walkers instead of sharing `flowir` + `refconfine` lock-region confinement.

## Soundness Constraints & Resolution Scope

1. **Chunk 5 — `flowir` Completion, Per-Package Body Cache & `summary_*` Unification**:
   - Update `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-init-aliasing` and `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-mutation-shape` in `docs/specs/closure.md` (re-pinning `.stipulator/bindings/closure.textproto`), extend `flowir` (`*ast.GenDecl` in `LowerNode`, `Place.RootPos`, `(*ValCall).CalleeSelector()`), cache lowered `*flowir.Body` + `ScopedMemAliases` per declaration in `FactOfWithHooks`, migrate `summary_uses.go` and `summary_uses_sync_opaque.go` onto `flowir` sharing `localCarrierAliases{root, all}` + `placeEntersInterior` + `BuildScopedMemAliases` (preserving exact `FactOfWithHooks` site-hook parity for `explain.go`), unify the expression purity/freshness/non-smuggling classifier onto `*flowir.Value`, finish migrating `summary_env_stores.go` and `summary_env_returns.go` off raw `go/ast`, and factor `propagateBodyVarTaint` in `summary_leakfree.go`. Leave `internal/seams` isolated on ordered AST statement spines.
2. **Chunk 6 — `ReadOnlyVarAliases`, Attestation-Free `StaticCompositeGlobals` & Fixpoint Consolidation**:
   - Update `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state`, `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-audited-discharges`, and `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-proto` in `docs/specs/closure.md` and `REQ-vouch-recorded` in `docs/specs/purity.md` (re-pinning `.stipulator/bindings/{closure,vouch}.textproto`), keeping `Compose` and `NonVouchDischargedVars` (`explain.go`, `REQ-explain-empty`) in lockstep.
   - **`ReadOnlyVarAliases` invariants**: Intersected across compilation variants; restricted to pointer-sharing single-definition `*NamedStruct` `var V = targetVar` initializers (`&targetVar` on a by-value `NamedStruct` `StaticCompositeGlobals` root is likewise admissible) with zero intra- or cross-package writes/rebinds/whole-root address-takes (`DirectVarWrites`) and intra-package pointer-root subplace confinement on `V`; propagates **only** binary-wide type-guarded capsule/composite discharges (`ProtoClusters`/`InitSettledClusterFact`, `StaticCompositeGlobals`, and `OnceMemoCandidates`, transitively across alias chains) — never use-site-verified mutable container discharges (`MappingDischarges`, `MemoDischarges`, `HarnessDischarges`), `PoolDischarges`, caller `Vouches` (`REQ-vouch-discharge`), or `SingleSubjectVars` directives (`REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-directive`); strictly preserves the target's authority tier (attestation-free targets propagate attestation-free, while `singleSubject`-attested targets like `ProtoClusters`/`InitSettledClusterFact` propagate only under `singleSubject` and record every discharged alias in `attestedDischarged` / `SingleSubjectDischarges` per `REQ-vouch-recorded`); and for `OnceMemoCandidates` inheritance, requires both intra-package direct-method-call-only confinement on `V` and cross-package `NonMethodVarUses[V] == false` while exempting verified `ReadOnlyVarAliases` initializers from non-method-use revocation along the alias chain.
   - **`StaticCompositeGlobals` invariants**: Delete `GrpcServiceVars` (`protoaudit/grpc.go`) and discharge field-name-free `StaticCompositeGlobals` **attestation-free** across all execution modes (`grpcFP.SingleSubjectDischarges` becomes `""` while `Valid` holds) when intersected across compilation variants: root variable is a non-empty named struct or pointer thereto satisfying root/subplace use confinement (zero `DirectVarWrites`, zero intra-package direct/subplace writes or mutating builtins, zero subplace reslices, non-struct `&subplace`, or non-basic subplace call arguments, and zero whole-root `&V` on pointer roots, while permitting whole-root `&V` on by-value `NamedStruct` roots and read/call-argument escapes of `&V` / pointer-root `V`), and every reachable field/element recursively consists solely of (a) pure scalars, (b) by-value or pointer non-empty named structs, (c) arrays or slices of by-value non-empty named structs (with `s[i] = ...`, range-LHS overwrite, `append`, `copy`, `clear`, and `unsafe.SliceData` on `[]NamedElem` marking `NamedElem` in `MutatedStructTypes`, while `&s[i]` on non-empty `NamedElem` remains guarded by downstream `*NamedElem` tracking in `MutatedStructTypes`, rejecting unnamed non-struct containers like `[]func()`), (d) bare non-generic package-level `*types.Func`s (no stateful closures or bound method values), and (e) `nil` / typed-`nil` / immutable-constant interfaces (never non-nil carrier-capable concrete values hidden behind an interface), with every reached named struct absent from binary-wide `MutatedStructTypes`.
   - Factor `validateFactCoreSlices`, `validateFactDischargeSlices`, and `intersectVariantItems` in `fixpoint_compose.go` plus `addMapSet`/edge helpers in `fixpoint_env.go`.
3. **Chunk 7 — Unified `flowir` Memoizer Confinement & `discharges_mapping` Lock-Region Consolidation**:
