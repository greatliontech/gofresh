# Unified `prepareProgramRTA`, Execution-Agnostic `dynstate.Fact` Memos & `discharges_audited.go` Split

- **Status:** Open
- **Governing contract:** [`docs/specs/closure.md`](../specs/closure.md) (`REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state`, `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-pool`, `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-reachability`, `REQ-closure-dynamic-state-memo`, `REQ-closure-scan-memo`), [`docs/specs/guards.md`](../specs/guards.md), [`docs/specs/explain.md`](../specs/explain.md) (`REQ-explain-chain`)
- **Lands:** Chunk 2 in [`docs/plans/plan.md`](../plans/plan.md)

## Problem

1. **Multi-Concern `discharges_audited.go` and Duplicate Protected-Container Confinement (`internal/dynstate/discharges_audited.go`)**
   - [`internal/dynstate/discharges_audited.go`](../../internal/dynstate/discharges_audited.go) (`1,948` lines) bundles `MappingDischarges` (`lines 1–1062`) with `MemoDischarges` (`lines 1063–1948`) and maintains a duplicate `callMutatesProtectedContainer` helper (`lines 1810–1822`, identical to `refconfine.callMutatesSeedStructContainer`) instead of reusing `refconfine.VerifyProtectedStructOriginConfinement`.

2. **Conditional `progSummary` Gate and Missing Shared `provFP` Across `attributedReachableSetsOnce` and `binaryReachableUnion`**
   - In [`internal/closure/attribution_batch.go`](../../internal/closure/attribution_batch.go) (`attributedReachableSetsOnce` at lines 299–300 and `binaryReachableUnion` at lines 983–984), `enumerateCallerReferencesForProgram(b.prog, b.h.SelectionAudited())` is skipped unless `audited || len(openCandidates) > 0`, and both functions duplicate ~55 lines of `openCandidates` filtering, `subjectEnumerationClosure` unioning, `hasCancelCtx` seeding, and the `rta.Analyze` / `res.Extend` + `collectTestingHarnessCallbacks` delta-root fixpoint loop (`lines 285–336` vs. `971–1028`).
   - Worse, while `attributedReachableSetsOnce` (`lines 338–341`) constructs a shared `provFP` when `summary != nil`, `binaryReachableUnion` (`line 1032`) passes `nil` into `provenanceBFSWithCandidates` ([`internal/closure/attribution_provenance.go`](../../internal/closure/attribution_provenance.go) lines 1860–1885), causing per-call `getProvFP()` allocation and `resetDynamicMemos()` across `ComputeBinaryRootedFunctions` and whenever `summary == nil` (as observed in [`internal/closure/rooted.go`](../../internal/closure/rooted.go) where `getProvFP()` reset dynamic memos `3,207` times across `1,069` subjects).

3. **Execution-Mode Fact-Cache Fragmentation, Unchanged Mutable-Snapshot Recomputation & `scanfacts` Completeness**
   - `(s AnalysisScope) Facts()` ([`internal/closure/scope.go`](../../internal/closure/scope.go) lines 100–112) appends `|single-subject-execution` and `|package-process-execution`, and `dynstate.FactOf` gates `PoolDischarges` vs. unattested `sync.Pool` mutation/escape/`AttributedUses` recording on `singleSubject` (`SingleSubjectVars` is already populated unconditionally), fragmenting the `dynamicstate` disk and process caches across `GeneralExecution`, `PackageExecution`, and `SingleSubjectExecution` even though per-package AST/type `dynstate.Fact` derivation is independent of execution attestations (`PackageProcess` and `SingleSubject` only govern `dynstate.Compose` and culprit reachability discharge).
   - On `MutableLocalPackage` targets, `observeSnapshot` calls `observeView` twice (`first` and `second`) and skips memo reuse in `scanViewSubjectsWithMemo` ([`scan.go`](../../scan.go)) even when the package's source file digests and import cone are identical across both passes, causing `6x` `dynstate.FactOf` recomputations (`1m45s` on `gofresh/internal/closure`, `1m26s` on `stipulator/internal/backends/golang`).

## Soundness Constraints & Resolution Scope

1. **`discharges_audited.go` Decomposition & `refconfine` Reuse**:
   - Split [`internal/dynstate/discharges_audited.go`](../../internal/dynstate/discharges_audited.go) (`1,948` lines) at line 1063 into `discharges_mapping.go` (`MappingDischarges`) and `discharges_memo.go` (`MemoDischarges`), reuse `refconfine.VerifyProtectedStructOriginConfinement`, and delete duplicate `callMutatesProtectedContainer`.
2. **Unified `prepareProgramRTA` Across `attributedReachableSetsOnce` and `binaryReachableUnion`**:
   - Extract a single `prepareProgramRTA` helper in [`internal/closure/attribution_batch.go`](../../internal/closure/attribution_batch.go) that unconditionally populates `enumerateCallerReferencesForProgram`, runs the RTA + `collectTestingHarnessCallbacks` delta-root fixpoint, and supplies a shared subject-independent `provFP` across all entrypoints in [`internal/closure/rooted.go`](../../internal/closure/rooted.go) (`ComputeRootedFunctions` and `ComputeBinaryRootedFunctions`) and `observability.go`, bumping `rootedFunctionsStrategy`.
3. **`(AnalysisScope).Facts()` vs. `(AnalysisScope).Scan()` Separation, Digest-Keyed `localFactCache` & `scanfacts` Integrity**:
   - Update `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-pool`, `REQ-closure-shared-dynamic-state-reachability`, `REQ-closure-dynamic-state-memo`, and `REQ-closure-scan-memo` in [`docs/specs/closure.md`](../specs/closure.md) (and re-pin `.stipulator/bindings/closure.textproto`), drop `singleSubject` from `dynstate.FactOf(audited, p)` (retaining unconditional `SingleSubjectVars`, recording `PoolDischarges` alongside unattested `PoolMutates`/`PoolEscapes`/`PoolAttributedUses` unconditionally on `Fact`, applying `singleSubject` / `packageProcess` gating solely inside `dynstate.Compose`, and preserving `singleSubject` filtering of `sync.Pool`-discharged `hooks.Site` observations in `FactOfWithHooks` / `explainCulprit` in [`explain.go`](../../explain.go), `REQ-explain-chain`), and remove `|single-subject-execution` and `|package-process-execution` from `(s AnalysisScope) Facts()` ([`internal/closure/scope.go`](../../internal/closure/scope.go) lines 100–112), **while explicitly retaining `|single-subject-execution` and `|package-process-execution` on `(s AnalysisScope) Scan()` (`lines 117–125`)** so execution-mode-dependent `scanfacts` entries never cross into `GeneralExecution` (`REQ-closure-scan-memo`).
   - Key a bounded session-scoped `localFactCache` (never `processFactCache`) over the transitive import cone's live file byte digests + pinned `Pin`s + `factScope` + `listing` (using exported `closure.IsPinnedPackageDir`) so disk edits re-derive immediately (`TestDynamicStateLocalFactsDeriveFreshEachScan`) while unchanged `observeSnapshot` passes derive `FactOf` once.
   - Preserve whole-package `scanfacts` completeness in `scanViewSubjectsWithMemo` ([`scan.go`](../../scan.go)): never persist partially discharged sibling symbols into whole-package `scanfacts`, and bump `DynamicStateStrategy` and `scanEntryVersion`.
