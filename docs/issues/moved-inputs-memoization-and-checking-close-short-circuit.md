# Unmemoized `withMovedInputs` Manifest Hashing & `CheckingUnit.Close` Stale-Slot Re-Evaluation

- **Status:** Open
- **Governing contract:** [`docs/specs/evidence.md`](../specs/evidence.md) (`REQ-fresh-result-model`, `REQ-fresh-checking-unit`), [`docs/specs/runtime-inputs.md`](../specs/runtime-inputs.md), [`docs/specs/purity.md`](../specs/purity.md) (`REQ-purity-override`)
- **Lands:** Chunk 6 in [`docs/plans/plan.md`](../plans/plan.md)

## Problem

When checking or revalidating batch-produced witness records that share a common `Fingerprint.RuntimeInputs` manifest, duplicate manifest-evaluation loops and `O(subjects * manifest_paths)` mover-attribution calls dominate verification latency:

1. **Duplicate Manifest-Evaluation Loop Between `populateManifestDigests` and `MovedInputs`, Plus Unmemoized `withMovedInputs` Across Subjects (`internal/runtimeinput/manifest.go:146-191, 367-410`, `validate.go:475-485, 524`, `checking.go:228, 240, 540-541`)**
   - `MovedInputs` ([`internal/runtimeinput/manifest.go`](../../internal/runtimeinput/manifest.go) lines 367–410) is a full duplicate of `decode` + `normalizeEnvironment` + `populateManifestDigests` (`lines 146–191`), re-hashing every env var and path in the manifest even though `populateManifestDigests` already compares/overwrites each recorded `entry.Digest` during `Current` / `currentWithNormalizedEnvContext` (and `Adopt` at `internal/runtimeinput/observation.go` lines 469–483 likewise calls both back-to-back).
   - Worse, while `observeRecordInputs` (`checking.go` lines 109–133) and `compareAttachedObservations` (`validate.go` lines 510–521) memoize `v.runtimeCheck()` once per distinct manifest, `withMovedInputs` ([`validate.go`](../../validate.go) lines 475–485) has no per-manifest memoization and calls `movedInputsForView` from scratch on every record in `checkRecords` (`checking.go` lines 228, 240) and `CheckingUnit.close` (`lines 540–541`).
   - In a batch of `N` tests sharing one `RuntimeInputs` manifest (e.g., `361` subjects in `gofresh` sharing a `345`-path manifest, or `70` subjects in `stipulator/internal/cmd` sharing a `6,766`-path / `3.15 MiB` manifest), `CheckBatch` and `CheckingUnit` decode the base64url JSON manifest and `stat`/`sha256`-hash every path `N` times per round (`124,545` path evaluations per call in `gofresh`, and `~140 GiB` of `sha256` hashing per round in `stipulator`).

2. **`CheckingUnit.close` Runs Proof Selection, `withMovedInputs`, and `runtimeMotion` on Already-`Stale` Historical Slots Only to Overwrite the Verdict (`checking.go:523-542`)**
   - During opening candidate selection (`CheckingUnit.Check` / `CheckBatch`), `u.slots` accumulates every historical candidate variant checked across rounds, most of which were already judged `Stale` (`1,431` stale historical slots alongside `20` valid served slots in `gofresh`).
   - In `CheckingUnit.close` ([`checking.go`](../../checking.go) lines 523–542), every evaluated slot whose opening `slot.result.verdict.Status` was already `Stale` runs full proof selection (`ensureObservable`), `withMovedInputs`, and `runtimeMotion` inside `current.checkRecords`, only for lines 537–538 (`if slot.result.verdict.Status == Stale { result.verdict = slot.result.verdict }`) to overwrite and discard the recomputed verdict.

## Soundness Constraints & Resolution Scope

1. **Unified Single-Pass Digest + Mover Evaluation & Point-in-Time Memo Invariant**:
   - Compute `moved []string` directly inside `populateManifestDigests` ([`internal/runtimeinput/manifest.go`](../../internal/runtimeinput/manifest.go) lines 146–191) whenever an entry carries a non-empty recorded `entry.Digest` and the recomputed digest differs, returning `(State, []string, error)` via a companion helper (`CurrentWithMovers` / endpoint sidecar struct) **without** adding slice or mode fields to `runtimeinput.State` (which must remain a pure Go-comparable struct for `observed != state` at `validate.go` line 522 and `before == after` at `checking.go` line 138) so `Current`, `MovedInputs`, `Adopt`, `compareAttachedObservations`, and `withMovedInputs` perform **zero** second disk/SHA-256 passes on the production path (preserving stipulator-bound `Current` and `currentWithNormalizedEnvContext`).
   - For unit tests that inject synthetic `v.runtimeCurrent != nil` hooks, pass a pass-scoped `moverMemo` per single point-in-time check pass (`CheckBatch` or `CheckingUnit.close`, **never** across `before`/`after` or pre-run/post-run boundaries, preserving `REQ-purity-override` `producing-motion` / `guard-drift`).
2. **`CheckingUnit.close` Stale-Slot Fast Path Preserving Endpoint-Interval Closing (`REQ-fresh-checking-unit`)**:
   - Per `REQ-fresh-checking-unit` (pinned by stipulator-bound witness `TestCheckingUnitCandidatesAndLifecycle` at `integration_execution_test.go` lines 661–729, `.stipulator/bindings/fresh.textproto` lines 1895–1932), evaluated `Stale` slots **must** continue to participate in `observeRecordInputs` (`shared` endpoint cache — 1 call per distinct manifest in `before` and 1 in `after`, never via `records[i].refusal` which represents `!slot.result.evaluated` evidence-only refusals), while skipping proof selection (`ensureObservable`), `withMovedInputs`, and `runtimeMotion` for slots already holding a terminal opening `Stale` verdict.
